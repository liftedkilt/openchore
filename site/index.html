How it works

Must-do first. Bonus last.

Every chore sits in one of three tiers, and each tier waits for the one before it. So nobody skips the dishes to grab the fun 15-point chore.

  1. Required

    The non-negotiables. Brush teeth, feed the dog.

  2. Core

    The daily routine. Points are held until every required chore is done.

  3. Bonus

    Extras. They pay out once required and core are finished.

Time windows

Chores appear when it's time, grouped into morning, afternoon and evening.

Deadlines

After the deadline, a chore is blocked, worth nothing, or costs points. Your call.

Streaks

Days in a row with everything done, with bonus points at milestones you choose.

Savings goals

Kids save up for something big, alone or pooled with their siblings.

Photo proof

Ask for a photo on any chore, and approve it from your phone.

Vacation mode

Pause a kid's chores when they're sick or away. The schedule stays put.

Optional chores

Nice-to-dos, with or without points. Skipping one never breaks a streak or costs a thing.

Their own to-dos

Kids add their own reminders for today, a day on the calendar, or every week. No points, just a nudge.

Daily decay

If you want it, a small points charge when yesterday was left unfinished.

Made for each kid

One app. Three skins. Eight colours.

Every kid picks the look that's theirs. A five-year-old and a teenager see the same chores, the same one-tap check, and a screen that feels made for them.

Points that mean something

A rewards store kids save up for.

You decide what's on the shelf and what it costs: screen time, picking dinner, a trip for ice cream. Set different prices for different kids, limit stock, or hide rewards that don't fit.

Every point earned or spent is recorded, so there's never an argument about the balance.

For parents

Everyone's day at a glance.

Shared screens use a neutral look so no one's skin takes over. Sign in with your own PIN or single sign-on, approve photos, and see how the week went.

Optional AI

Smart extras, on your terms.

Everything works without AI. If you want it, connect any OpenAI-compatible model, on your own machine or hosted, and turn it off any time.

  • Photo notes. A vision model takes a first look at photo proof and leaves you a note. It never rejects a chore. You have the final say.
  • Weekly summaries of how each kid's week went.
  • Chore descriptions drafted for you.
  • Natural read-aloud voices for kids who are still learning to read.

Get started

Up and running in three commands.

All you need is Docker on a computer at home. Then open http://localhost:8080 and a setup wizard walks you through adding your family.

# grab it
git clone https://github.com/liftedkilt/openchore.git
cd openchore

# start it
docker compose up -d
  1. Add your family. Parents, kids, skins and colours.
  2. Pick some chores. Start from the presets or write your own.
  3. Put it on the wall. Open it on a tablet and choose Add to Home Screen.
