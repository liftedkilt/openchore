The shared "Who's here?" screen on a tablet, shown by day in **House** and at night in **House Dark**.

- Every person gets a door of the same size, radius and layout: avatar, name, what's left, points, and their day. Each door is drawn in that person's own skin: Emma uses Sunroom (sun arc), Lily Tint (her ring in mint) and Noah Blocks (shape row). Tapping a door opens that skin.
- The frame itself takes no skin's signature. It has a neutral ground and one neutral typeface, and colour comes only from people.
- Grown-ups share one House-styled door behind a PIN or linked account. It shows what's waiting for them (chores to check, rewards to hand out).
- The family's progress sits along the bottom in each person's colour, in family order, never ranked.
- At night the frame switches to House Dark. The doors keep their skins but dim, so none glares, and the copy follows the clock ("Good evening.", "All done").
