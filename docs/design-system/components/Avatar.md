A person's initial on their own colour, the most visible thread between skins.

- **Props:** `name`, `color` (`coral`, `mint`, `butter` or `sky`) and `size` (`sm` 32px, `md` 40px or `lg` 96px).
- The shape comes from the skin: a soft blob that differs per person in Sunroom (set by the name), a rounded square with an outline in Blocks, a circle in House and Tint.
- A person with no colour (a second grown-up, say) falls back to `surface-sunk`.
- Always pair it with the name in text nearby; the initial alone is not a label.
