The three tabs every skin shares: Today, Week and Rewards.

- **Props:** `active` (`today`, `week` or `rewards`). It positions itself at the bottom of the nearest positioned ancestor.
- Sunroom floats a pill with labels. Blocks runs a full-width bar with a yolk pill. Tint shows icons only, with a dot in the person's colour.
- Never add, remove or reorder tabs per skin.
